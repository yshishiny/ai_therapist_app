# Clinical Agent - Note Templates

## SOAP Note Template

- **Subjective**: Patient's report of current symptoms, feelings, and experiences.
- **Objective**: Observed behavior, mental status exam, and test results.
- **Assessment**: Clinical interpretation of the session and progress toward goals.
- **Plan**: Next steps, homework, and focus for the upcoming session.

## DAP Note Template

- **Data**: Objective facts and patient observations.
- **Assessment**: Professional evaluation of the data.
- **Plan**: Future directions and interventions.
