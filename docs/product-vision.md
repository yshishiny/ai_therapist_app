# Product Vision

## Purpose
AI Therapist is evolving from a clinician and patient application into a multi-tenant therapist SaaS platform.

## Primary users
- Platform owner
- Practice admin
- Clinician
- Supervisor
- Patient

## Product goals
- Give therapy practices a secure clinical operating system
- Provide controlled access to assessments, content, patients, and workflows
- Enable patient login for assessments, homework, and guided engagement
- Support subscription and pay-as-you-go monetization
- Protect licensed and proprietary content such as books, guidelines, and tools

## Commercial model
- Base subscription per therapist or practice
- Metered usage for premium AI and premium catalog items
- Add-ons for premium assessment packs, content packs, branding, analytics, and supervisor workflows

## Core principles
- Backend-enforced permissions
- Tenant isolation by organisation
- Clinical safety and auditability
- Controlled rollout without breaking existing patient and clinician flows
