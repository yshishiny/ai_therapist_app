# AI Therapist — Mindfulness & Adjunct Techniques Summary

**Purpose**: Clinical workflow planning. Not a diagnostic tool.  
**Criteria**:

- **Evidence**: Strength of RCT support (High/Moderate/Developing)
- **Speed**: Time to measurable benefit (Immediate/Short-term/Program)
- **Efficiency**: Benefit relative to therapist time

## Clinical Workflow: The 4 Steps

1. **Screen & Fit**: Identify goal (sleep vs panic vs shame) and risks (dissociation?).
2. **Choose & Launch**: Pick _one_ starter technique (1-3 min).
3. **Teach (Micro-Script)**: Explain purpose -> Demonstrate -> Do 1 rep -> Debrief.
4. **Assign Homework**: Frequency > Duration (e.g., 2 min daily).
