## 1. MBSR School (Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction)

**Origin**: Jon Kabat-Zinn, UMass Medical School  
**Best for**: Stress, anxiety, chronic pain, general wellbeing  
**Evidence**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Extensive RCT support)

### Techniques

| Technique              | Duration  | Speed  | Best For                   |
| ---------------------- | --------- | ------ | -------------------------- |
| **Body Scan**          | 20-45 min | Medium | Tension, somatic awareness |
| **Sitting Meditation** | 10-30 min | Medium | Focus, equanimity          |
| **Walking Meditation** | 10-20 min | Fast   | Restlessness, grounding    |
| **Mindful Movement**   | 15-30 min | Fast   | Body reconnection          |
| **Informal Practice**  | Ongoing   | Fast   | Daily integration          |

#### Body Scan Script (Short: 3 min)

```
Settle into a comfortable position. Close your eyes.
Bring attention to your feet... notice any sensations.
Move up to your legs... just observing, not changing.
Notice your belly... chest... hands... arms...
Notice your shoulders... neck... face... head...
Take three breaths, feeling your whole body.
When ready, gently open your eyes.
```

## 2. MBCT School (Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy)

**Origin**: Segal, Williams, Teasdale  
**Best for**: Depression relapse prevention, rumination  
**Evidence**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Strong RCT for depression relapse)

### Techniques

| Technique                               | Duration  | Speed     | Best For                          |
| --------------------------------------- | --------- | --------- | --------------------------------- |
| **3-Minute Breathing Space**            | 3 min     | Immediate | Acute distress, pattern interrupt |
| **Thoughts Are Not Facts**              | 5-10 min  | Fast      | Decentering, cognitive fusion     |
| **Pleasant/Unpleasant Events Calendar** | Daily     | Medium    | Awareness patterns                |
| **Mindful Movement + Depression Signs** | 15-30 min | Medium    | Relapse awareness                 |

#### 3-Minute Breathing Space Script

```
STEP 1 (1 min): AWARENESS
What am I experiencing right now?
Thoughts? Feelings? Body sensations?
Just notice, acknowledge.

STEP 2 (1 min): GATHERING
Narrow attention to the breath.
Follow each inhale and exhale.
Use breath as anchor.

STEP 3 (1 min): EXPANDING
Expand awareness to whole body.
Include any difficulty with acceptance.
Carry this awareness forward.
```

## 3. DBT Mindfulness School

**Origin**: Marsha Linehan  
**Best for**: Emotion dysregulation, BPD traits, self-harm, impulsivity  
**Evidence**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Strong for BPD, emotion regulation)

### Core Skills

| Skill                | Type | Speed     | Best For                   |
| -------------------- | ---- | --------- | -------------------------- |
| **Wise Mind**        | Core | Immediate | Decision-making, balance   |
| **Observe**          | What | Fast      | Awareness without reaction |
| **Describe**         | What | Fast      | Labeling experience        |
| **Participate**      | What | Medium    | Full engagement            |
| **Non-judgmentally** | How  | Medium    | Shame, self-criticism      |
| **One-mindfully**    | How  | Fast      | Multitasking, overwhelm    |
| **Effectively**      | How  | Fast      | Goal-focus                 |

#### Wise Mind Script (2 min)

```
Find a comfortable position.
Notice your breath naturally.

Imagine a line between Emotion Mind (hot, impulsive)
and Reasonable Mind (cold, logical).

Right in the middle is Wise Mind.
It includes both emotion and logic.
It's the part of you that "just knows."

Ask yourself: What does my Wise Mind say about this?
Wait. Listen. Trust what arises.
```

## 4. ACT School (Acceptance & Commitment Therapy)

**Origin**: Steven Hayes  
**Best for**: Avoidance, anxiety, OCD, chronic pain, values work  
**Evidence**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Strong transdiagnostic evidence)

### Six Processes

| Process              | Technique                        | Speed     | Best For                   |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------- | -------------------------- |
| **Defusion**         | "I'm having the thought that..." | Immediate | Cognitive fusion           |
| **Acceptance**       | Making Room                      | Medium    | Experiential avoidance     |
| **Present Moment**   | Contact exercises                | Fast      | Dissociation, future worry |
| **Self-as-Context**  | Observer Self                    | Medium    | Identity fusion            |
| **Values**           | Values Clarification             | Medium    | Direction, meaning         |
| **Committed Action** | Behavioral goals                 | Ongoing   | Avoidance patterns         |

#### Cognitive Defusion Script (2 min)

```
Notice a difficult thought you're having.
Instead of "I'm worthless," say:
"I'm having the thought that I'm worthless."

Now try: "I notice I'm having the thought that..."

Even further: "My mind is telling me the story that..."

The thought is still there—but you're watching it.
You are not the thought. You're the one noticing.
```

## 5. Compassion School

**Origin**: Kristin Neff, Paul Gilbert, Sharon Salzberg  
**Best for**: Shame, self-criticism, trauma recovery, attachment wounds  
**Evidence**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Growing RCT support)

### Techniques

| Technique                   | Duration  | Speed     | Best For              |
| --------------------------- | --------- | --------- | --------------------- |
| **Self-Compassion Break**   | 3-5 min   | Immediate | Acute self-criticism  |
| **Loving-Kindness (Metta)** | 10-20 min | Medium    | Warmth, connection    |
| **Compassionate Imagery**   | 5-15 min  | Medium    | Inner critic work     |
| **Compassionate Letter**    | 15-30 min | Medium    | Deep shame processing |

#### Self-Compassion Break Script (3 min)

```
STEP 1: MINDFULNESS
"This is a moment of suffering."
(Acknowledge the pain without drowning in it)

STEP 2: COMMON HUMANITY
"Suffering is part of life. I'm not alone in this."
(Connect to shared human experience)

STEP 3: KINDNESS
Place hand on heart.
"May I be kind to myself."
"May I give myself the compassion I need."
(Offer warmth to yourself)
```

## 6. Somatic/Trauma-Informed School

**Origin**: Peter Levine, Pat Ogden, Bessel van der Kolk  
**Best for**: Trauma, dissociation, body disconnection, panic  
**Evidence**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Growing trauma-specific evidence)

> ⚠️ **Trauma Warning**: Use gentler grounding first. Avoid deep body awareness if client is dissociating or flooded.

### Techniques

| Technique                | Duration  | Speed     | Best For                   |
| ------------------------ | --------- | --------- | -------------------------- |
| **5-4-3-2-1 Grounding**  | 2-5 min   | Immediate | Dissociation, panic        |
| **Orienting**            | 2-5 min   | Immediate | Hyperarousal               |
| **Safe Place Anchoring** | 5-10 min  | Fast      | Stabilization              |
| **Pendulation**          | 10-20 min | Medium    | Titrated trauma processing |
| **Containment**          | 5-10 min  | Fast      | Overwhelming material      |

#### 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Script (2 min)

```
Look around and name:
5 things you can SEE (colors, objects, details)
4 things you can TOUCH (textures, temperature)
3 things you can HEAR (near and far sounds)
2 things you can SMELL (or imagine smelling)
1 thing you can TASTE (or notice in your mouth)

Take a breath. Feel your feet on the floor.
You are here, now, safe in this moment.
```

## Contraindications & Safer Alternatives

| Risk              | Avoid                          | Use Instead                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Dissociation      | Long body scans, eyes closed   | 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, eyes open      |
| Panic history     | Breath focus                   | External focus (sounds, objects)    |
| Trauma flashbacks | Deep body awareness            | Orienting, containment              |
| Psychosis         | Meditation retreats, long sits | Brief, structured, therapist-guided |
| Severe depression | Solo practice                  | Therapist-guided, brief practices   |
