## Tier 2: Personality Profiling (DSM-5 AMPD)

| Version         | Items | Age   | License    |
| --------------- | ----- | ----- | ---------- |
| PID-5 Full      | 220   | Adult | FREE (APA) |
| PID-5 Brief     | 25    | Adult | FREE (APA) |
| PID-5 Informant | 220   | Adult | FREE (APA) |
| PID-5 Child     | 220   | 11-17 | FREE (APA) |

**Domains measured** (DSM-5 Alternative Model):

- Negative Affectivity
- Detachment
- Antagonism
- Disinhibition
- Psychoticism

**Workflow**:

1. Start with PID-5 Brief (screening)
2. Use PID-5 Full when traits are clinically relevant
3. Combine with Criterion A (functioning) for full AMPD assessment

## Recommended Intake Sequence

```
1. Level-1 Cross-Cutting (5-10 min)
   ↓
2. Level-2 for flagged domains (5-15 min)
   ↓
3. WHODAS 2.0 functioning (5 min)
   ↓
4. CFI if cultural factors relevant (10-15 min)
   ↓
5. PID-5 Brief if personality traits indicated (5 min)
```

## Implementation Notes

### What you CAN digitize (Google Forms / in-app):

- Level-1 and Level-2 measures (APA PDFs permit clinical use)
- PID-5 (APA explicitly allows reproduction for clinical use)
- WHODAS 2.0 (WHO permits use)
- PHQ-9, GAD-7, PSS-10, WHO-5 (widely free)

### What you should NOT digitize publicly:

- SCID-5 interviews (licensed)
- BDI-II (licensed)
- MMPI (licensed)
- Any proprietary personality tests
