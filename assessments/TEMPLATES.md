## PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9) — FREE

**Purpose**: Depression screening  
**Scoring**: 0-27 (sum of 9 items, 0-3 each)

| Score | Severity          |
| ----- | ----------------- |
| 0-4   | Minimal           |
| 5-9   | Mild              |
| 10-14 | Moderate          |
| 15-19 | Moderately Severe |
| 20-27 | Severe            |

**Item 9 flag**: If >0, assess suicide risk immediately.

---

## GAD-7 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7) — FREE

**Purpose**: Anxiety screening  
**Scoring**: 0-21 (sum of 7 items, 0-3 each)

| Score | Severity |
| ----- | -------- |
| 0-4   | Minimal  |
| 5-9   | Mild     |
| 10-14 | Moderate |
| 15-21 | Severe   |

## PSS-10 (Perceived Stress Scale) — FREE

**Purpose**: Stress level  
**Scoring**: 0-40 (reverse score items 4,5,7,8)

| Score | Interpretation  |
| ----- | --------------- |
| 0-13  | Low stress      |
| 14-26 | Moderate stress |
| 27-40 | High stress     |

---

## WHO-5 (Well-Being Index) — FREE

**Purpose**: Wellbeing screening  
**Scoring**: 0-25 (sum × 4 = percentage)

| Score | Interpretation                        |
| ----- | ------------------------------------- |
| ≤50%  | Poor wellbeing, screen for depression |
| >50%  | Adequate wellbeing                    |

---

## PCL-5 (PTSD Checklist) — VERIFY

**Purpose**: PTSD screening  
**Scoring**: 0-80
**Cutoff**: ≥31-33 suggests probable PTSD

---

## ACE (Adverse Childhood Experiences) — FREE

**Purpose**: Trauma history  
**Scoring**: 0-10 count of adverse experiences

| Score | Risk                                          |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 0-3   | Baseline                                      |
| ≥4    | Elevated risk for health/mental health issues |
